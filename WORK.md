# WORK.md

## Mission
By **Monday, 16 March 2026**, have a **finished, saleable software product** ready to show, demo, and pitch to real businesses in **Al Quoz, Dubai**.

This sprint is about **getting to market**, not building everything.

Primary offer for this sprint:

- **Front-end sell:** WhatsApp + Website + Google + Call flow improvement
- **Back-end product:** **Sovereign Workshop Control v1**

Core sales message:

> We help workshops get more enquiries, respond better, track jobs better, and keep photo proof and customer updates organised in one system.

---

## Non-Negotiables
Every day must include:

- **Build** something that makes the demo stronger
- **Package** something that makes the offer easier to understand
- **Reach out** to real businesses
- **Learn** from real reactions and objections

Minimum daily numbers:

- 3 new prospects added
- 3 follow-ups sent
- 1 improvement made to the offer
- 1 market lesson written down

---

## v1 Product Boundary
### Included in v1
- Lead capture
- Customer records
- Vehicle records
- Job cards
- Status tracking
- Notes
- Photo evidence
- Simple proof export

### Not included in v1
- Full accounting
- Full invoicing system
- Full parts inventory
- Advanced AI features
- Complex staff permissions

---

## Target Customer
- Small to mid-size car workshops in **Al Quoz**
- Businesses currently relying on **WhatsApp, calls, memory, scattered files, and manual follow-up**

## Top Pain Points
1. WhatsApp chaos
2. Missed calls / weak follow-up
3. No clean job tracking
4. Scattered photos and evidence
5. Weak Google / trust presence
